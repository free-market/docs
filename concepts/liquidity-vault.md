# Liquidity Vault

### Overview

The Liquidity Vault is a dedicated on-chain liquidity pool designed to support Pathfinder’s yield stacking operations. It functions as an internal reserve that loans a portion of user funds to the Brevity Engine—ensuring that active yield strategies have immediate access to liquid assets. Additionally, external users can stake stablecoins (e.g., USDC) in the vault to earn Pathfinder tokens, further deepening liquidity and aligning incentives across the ecosystem.

### How It Works

* **Internal Liquidity Reserve:**\
  When users deposit stablecoins into Pathfinder, up to 20% of those funds are automatically allocated to the Liquidity Vault. These funds are then “loaned” to the Brevity Engine to facilitate rapid, automated unwinding of the yield stack when a withdrawal is initiated. This ensures that the entire pipeline can be liquidated instantly, preserving the active yield mechanism’s promise of near-instant liquidity.
* **Tokenized Representation:**\
  Users (and external liquidity providers) who stake in the Liquidity Vault receive a tokenized representation of their share. These tokens confer rights to a portion of the fee revenue generated by the system, creating an additional yield stream. The tokens also play a role in governance and incentive alignment within the broader Pathfinder network.
* **Automated Rebalancing & Hooks:**\
  In active yield mode, pre-transaction hooks pull funds from the Liquidity Vault to quickly satisfy withdrawal requests. After the transaction, post-transaction hooks re-sweep any excess funds back into the vault, ensuring that the liquidity reserve is continuously replenished. This automated mechanism optimizes the efficiency of the yield stacking process and minimizes latency.

### Benefits

* **Instant Liquidity:**\
  The Liquidity Vault provides a dedicated, fast-access reserve that guarantees users can withdraw their funds on-demand, even if the assets are deployed across multiple yield layers.
* **Enhanced Yield Optimization:**\
  By centralizing a portion of user funds in the vault, Pathfinder ensures that active yield operations can efficiently rebalance and redeploy assets, maximizing compound returns.
* **Incentivized Participation:**\
  External users can stake stablecoins into the vault, earning Pathfinder tokens as rewards. This not only boosts overall liquidity but also drives network effects as token holders gain governance rights and revenue-sharing benefits.
* **Risk Mitigation:**\
  By acting as an independent liquidity source, the vault reduces the risk of slippage or liquidity shortfalls during high-demand periods, ensuring the overall stability of the yield stack.
