# Onboarding/ asset conversion

## Onboarding and Asset Conversion

Pathfinder simplifies the process of transforming various asset types into yield-bearing positions. We support a wide range of assets, ensuring that whether you hold crypto, stablecoins, cash, or real-world assets (RWAs), your funds can be seamlessly onboarded into our yield pipeline.

### Crypto and Stablecoins

For digital assets—including cryptocurrencies and stablecoins—we integrate with **fun.xyz**. This platform provides an automated, user-friendly gateway that:

* **Converts and Aggregates:** Quickly converts deposited crypto and stablecoins into compatible yield tokens.
* **Streamlined Onboarding:** Offers a simple user interface that connects directly to Pathfinder, so users can deposit their digital assets with minimal friction.
* **Seamless Integration:** Automatically transfers assets into the stacked yield pipeline, enabling users to immediately start earning compounded passive or active yield.

### Cash

Traditional fiat cash is also supported through our partnership with **Bridge/Stripe**:

* **Fiat Conversion:** When users deposit cash, Bridge/Stripe handles the secure and compliant conversion of fiat currency into stablecoins.
* **Automated Routing:** Once converted, these stablecoins are seamlessly funneled into Pathfinder’s yield pipeline, ensuring that even traditional cash can earn competitive returns.
* **Unified Experience:** Users benefit from a consistent yield experience, regardless of whether they begin with cash or digital assets.

### Real-World Assets

For assets that represent real-world value, such as bonds or securitized instruments, Pathfinder integrates with **Securitize**:

* **Tokenization of RWAs:** Securitize facilitates the conversion of real-world assets into blockchain-based tokens.
* **Automated Yield Deployment:** These tokenized RWAs are then automatically moved into dedicated yield strategies within Pathfinder.
* **Broadened Asset Base:** This integration expands the range of assets that can earn yield, allowing platforms to tap into the growing market for on-chain real-world asset yields.
